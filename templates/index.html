What is a Constraint Satisfaction Problem?
Formal AI Formulation ⟨X, D, C⟩

In Artificial Intelligence, a CSP represents computational problems as a set of variables that must be assigned values satisfying declarative constraints.

  • Variables (Χ): Discrete production tasks requiring allocation.
  • Domains (Δ): Cartesian product of allowable (Machine, Worker, Time Slot) tuples.
  • Constraints (C): 10 Hard logical requirements (zero temporal overlaps, certified operator skills, shift hours, inventory stock, and deadlines).
How AI Helps Production Planning
Tackling Combinatorial Complexity

Manual scheduling suffers from combinatorial explosion ($O(D^N)$), where search space grows exponentially with orders, leading to machine idle time, operator double-booking, and missed customer deadlines.

Our Python AI engine applies Minimum Remaining Values (MRV) and Forward Checking to prune invalid search branches high up in the decision tree, deriving mathematically proven, conflict-free schedules in under 5 milliseconds.

Core Features
Recursive Backtracking

Full state-space exploration algorithm that incrementally commits variable values and backtracks systematically upon detecting constraint breaches.

MRV Heuristic

Chooses the most constrained task first ("Fail-First" principle), minimizing total search steps and avoiding fruitless downstream evaluations.

Dynamic Rescheduling

Instantly recalculates alternative feasible schedules when machinery enters maintenance or operators take leave.

10 Hard Constraints

Rigorous validation of machine non-overlap, worker non-overlap, skills, shift hours, raw material stock, and delivery deadlines.

Explainable AI (XAI)

Displays human-readable justification cards for every scheduling decision and root-cause conflict diagnostics when unsolvable.

Audit Trails & Export

Persists constraint evaluations to SQLite audit logs and enables one-click CSV export and printable shopfloor dispatch sheets.
